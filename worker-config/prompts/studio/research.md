## Phase: Research (docs/RESEARCH.md)

Research the market and the users with web search and fetch. Every factual claim gets a source link; no source, no claim.
Cover:
- **Competitors** (5-10): what each does well and badly, pricing, platforms, a link. A comparison table.
- **What users say**: patterns from app-store reviews, Reddit and forum threads (quote short phrases with links). Pains
  that keep coming up are the most valuable finding.
- **Gaps and angle**: where this idea could be clearly better for the people in the brief, or why it can't.
- **Platform and technical facts that matter** (APIs, data access, store rules), only where they change the decision.
- **Risks**: legal, platform, data, cost.

End with **Go / no-go**, and set `"verdict"` in your status file:
- `build`: worth building as briefed; say why in two lines.
- `narrow`: worth building with a sharper angle; give that angle.
- `dont`: not worth it; say what to use instead (an existing app, a setup, a script), honestly. Saving the owner's
  time is a good outcome.
Keep the document scannable: a summary at the top, details below.

Interview: research is mostly your own work. Ask only what changes where you look: which competitors the owner already
uses or knows, which markets and languages matter, and whether to look at adjacent products. One short round at most.
